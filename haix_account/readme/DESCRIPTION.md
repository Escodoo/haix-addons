Finance customizations for Haix.

- **Due date lock**: the due date of receivables and payables cannot be changed directly;
  a reschedule keeps the original due date and records the expected payment date.
- **Vendor bills from purchases**: a vendor bill must come from a purchase order, except for
  users in the group allowed to post vendor bills without a purchase.
- **Measurement approver and billing contact** on the sale order.
- **Credit control**: the follow-up history is kept on the customer.
- **Cash flow forecast** (`mis_builder_cash_flow`) uses the expected payment date.
