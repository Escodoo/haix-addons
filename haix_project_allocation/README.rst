This module lets the scheduling team allocate field employees to project
tasks by day and shift (morning, afternoon, night, on-call), check who
is available in a given period, and see a timeline per person.

Shift hours come from the employee's working schedule (morning and
afternoon) or from fixed hours set on the shift (night and on-call).
Approved and pending time off and public holidays count as absences.

Configuration
=============

1. Give the scheduling team the *Allocation: Scheduling* access right
   (Settings > Users). Users who only need to see the planning get
   *Allocation: Read*. The scheduling team also needs the *Employees:
   Officer* right to read time off and working schedules.
2. Mark the field employees in Project > Configuration > Team Allocation
   > Field Team (or in the employee form, *Field Team* option). Only
   these employees can be allocated.
3. Check the working schedule and the timezone of each field employee:
   the morning and afternoon shifts take their hours from it.
4. Create the teams in Project > Configuration > Team Allocation > Field
   Teams, with their usual leader and members.
5. Review the shifts in Project > Configuration > Team Allocation >
   Shifts. Night and on-call have fixed hours: adjust them to the
   company's rules.
6. In Project > Configuration > Settings, *Block Overlapping
   Allocations* (enabled by default) prevents an employee from having
   two overlapping allocations. When disabled, overlaps are allowed and
   shown in red.

Usage
=====

**Allocate a team to a task**

1. Open a task and click *Allocate Team*.
2. Choose the days, the shifts (*Full Day* selects morning and
   afternoon) and, optionally, the team of the site.
3. The available employees are listed first, and the team members are
   preselected. *Show Unavailable* also lists who is allocated or
   absent, with the reason.
4. Confirm: one allocation is created per employee, day and shift.

**See who is available**

1. Go to Project > Allocation > Available in Period.
2. Choose the period and the shifts, then filter by team or department.
3. Select employees and click *Allocate* to allocate them to a task, or
   *See Planning* to open the timeline for the period.

**Follow the planning**

Project > Allocation > Team Planning shows one line per employee in the
timeline. Drag a bar to another day or to another employee to move the
allocation. Click a bar to open the allocation. Allocations in conflict
are shown in red.

**Replace an employee**

1. Open the allocation of the employee who will not come and click
   *Substitute Employee*.
2. Choose the substitute among the candidates for that day and shift.
3. If the substitute is allocated elsewhere at that time, the other
   allocation is archived by default. Inform the reason: it is logged in
   the chatter of the allocations and of the task involved.
