This module lets the scheduling team allocate field employees to project
tasks by day and shift (morning, afternoon, night, on-call), check who
is available in a given period, and see a timeline per person.

Shift hours come from the employee's working schedule (morning and
afternoon) or from fixed hours set on the shift (night and on-call).
Approved and pending time off and public holidays count as absences.
