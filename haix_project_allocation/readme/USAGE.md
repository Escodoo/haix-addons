**Allocate a team to a task**

1.  Open a task and click *Allocate Team*.
2.  Choose the days, the shifts (*Full Day* selects morning and
    afternoon) and, optionally, the team of the site.
3.  The available employees are listed first, and the team members are
    preselected. *Show Unavailable* also lists who is allocated or
    absent, with the reason.
4.  Confirm: one allocation is created per employee, day and shift.

**See who is available**

1.  Go to Project \> Allocation \> Available in Period.
2.  Choose the period and the shifts, then filter by team or
    department.
3.  Select employees and click *Allocate* to allocate them to a task, or
    *See Planning* to open the timeline for the period.

**Follow the planning**

Project \> Allocation \> Team Planning shows one line per employee in
the timeline. Drag a bar to another day or to another employee to move
the allocation. Click a bar to open the allocation. Allocations in
conflict are shown in red.

**Replace an employee**

1.  Open the allocation of the employee who will not come and click
    *Substitute Employee*.
2.  Choose the substitute among the candidates for that day and shift.
3.  If the substitute is allocated elsewhere at that time, the other
    allocation is archived by default. Inform the reason: it is logged in
    the chatter of the allocations and of the task involved.
