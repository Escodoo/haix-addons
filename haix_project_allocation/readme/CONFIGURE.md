1.  Give the scheduling team the *Allocation: Scheduling* access right
    (Settings \> Users). Users who only need to see the planning get
    *Allocation: Read*. The scheduling team also needs the *Employees:
    Officer* right to read time off and working schedules.
2.  Mark the field employees in Project \> Configuration \> Team
    Allocation \> Field Team (or in the employee form, *Field Team*
    option). Only these employees can be allocated.
3.  Check the working schedule and the timezone of each field employee:
    the morning and afternoon shifts take their hours from it.
4.  Create the teams in Project \> Configuration \> Team Allocation \>
    Field Teams, with their usual leader and members.
5.  Review the shifts in Project \> Configuration \> Team Allocation \>
    Shifts. Night and on-call have fixed hours: adjust them to the
    company's rules.
6.  In Project \> Configuration \> Settings, *Block Overlapping Allocations* (enabled by
    default) prevents an employee from having two overlapping
    allocations. When disabled, overlaps are allowed and shown in red.
