Stock and rental logistics customizations for Haix, on top of
``sale_rental``.

-  **Products**: a unique number for each product, the asset type
   (rental equipment or assembly supply), whether the item must be
   inspected when it comes back, and three values (indemnity, used sale
   and supplier).
-  **Inspection and maintenance**: returned items that require
   inspection go to the inspection location; the product shows the
   quantities in inspection and in maintenance.
-  **Lease sheet**: the analytic account of the rental order (lease
   sheet) is propagated to the rental deliveries, returns and their
   moves.
-  **Return date**: a group allowed to reschedule only the return date
   of a rental return.
-  **Return slip**: report to print the return slip from the transfer.
-  **Missing items**: "Charge Missing Items" on the sale order adds an
   indemnity line for each rented item that went to the customer and did
   not come back, at its indemnity value.
-  **Rental dates** are set at noon, so they do not move to the day
   before because of the time zone.
